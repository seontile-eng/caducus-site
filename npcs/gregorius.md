# Gregorius

### Overt Goals

Publicly champions Caducus as a scholarly outpost for young magi, extolling the region's magical
potential and its value as a vantage to investigate and challenge the **Order of Odin**. Frames it
as a place far from the Rhine's center where spirited young magi study and grow "without inflaming
tensions."
