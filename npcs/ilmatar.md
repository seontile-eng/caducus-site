# Ilmatar

### As the world knows them

Matriarch of the sprawling Koroinen kin near Turku; a renowned and feared healer-witch of the Aurajoki ("Pine-Whisper Sorceress of the Aurajoki," Reputation 3 Local). Stands at the uneasy crossroads between the mortal clans, the encroaching Swedish and Novgorodian arms, and the new Hermetic covenant of Caducus. Wife of war-leader Väinö, whom she loves fiercely but contests in many things. ~60 winters, but moves like a woman half her age.

### Appearance
Breathtaking: flax-gold hair shot with birch-silver, a face unlined by age. **Huithapeli (faerie) blood** marks her — delicate velvet-dark bud-antlers studded with iridescent pearl-nodes sprout from her brow, shifting hue with mood (spring-green when she laughs, ember-red when angered). Undyed wool gown; bare feet hardened like horn. Pre +2 generally; sexually compatible people tend to swoon (Venus' Blessing → effective Pre +5).

### Demeanour & speech (for voicing her)
Acerbic Sage (razor insight in one-word verdicts or crooked proverbs; feelings are collateral); Incomprehensible (oblique, riddling — verbs tangle, nouns sprout roots; −3 to teaching/writing/explaining); Merry Trickster (harmless pranks — swaps drinking horns, turns waystones, knots manes; never genuine harm); Feral Courtesy (dislikes halls/courts/sermons; vanishes mid-conversation when bored); Family First (patience for children, nieces, shy servants; legendary scorn of lords). Begins in medias res ("When the antler breaks — then count arrows, eh?"); ends on disruptive questions ("Why cage a goose that walks back each spring?").

### Folk Witchcraft & lore
Healing Mistress (cura animarum unsurpassed locally — bone-mending poultices, chants that turn gangrene to milk-pale scar; also versed in the other folk-witch magics); Scholar of the Unspoken (deep knowledge of local sprites and haltijas, though her teaching collapses into pantomime). "Iron-Whisper Resonance": her casting rings with swirling bog-iron filings; nearby oak leaves briefly pattern a triple-leaf footprint on the ground.

### Current concerns (live hooks)
1. **Rise of Iron Law:** Swedish/Novgorodian threats empower Turku's menfolk to monopolise defence as a male prerogative; she agitates for matrilineal power and seeks Hermetic allies willing to anchor it.
2. **Väinö's Restless Sword:** she opposes his plan to abdicate leadership, fearing idleness will dull his edge and hasten death; she wishes she could brew him a longevity draught (not just for herself) and will try to persuade the magi to provide one if she learns it's possible.
3. **Hidden Vis Stores & Sources:** years have fattened her coffers; she bargains shrewdly, yielding only enough vis to appease the Order while masking richer lodes in hollow oaks upriver. She especially withholds **Corpus**, which she needs for healing.
4. **Caducus Gamble:** convinced for now that welcoming the Hermetic magi is beneficial on the whole, she nonetheless prepares for worse outcomes.
