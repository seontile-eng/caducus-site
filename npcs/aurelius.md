# Aurelius

*Lean scholar-magus who looks ~30, tired and disheveled, short brown hair, piercing green eyes, caught mid-speech and gesturing; indigo hooded wool robe, a silver medallion on a chain with an obscure symbol. Gallen-Kallela matte oil portrait.*

### Overt Goals

Says he aims to expand the Order's horizons and empower the next generation; wants Caducus to be a
place where young magi study with greater freedom and fewer constraints. He praises collaboration
and open exchange of ideas — but is careful never to name the power-hoarding of the elder magi,
preferring to keep such themes abstract.

### Presence & Reach

The Pontifex attends **council** — the Spring Council above all — and is otherwise seldom at the covenant in person. Day to day he is reached through the **twinned quills** (Durenmar's founding gift): write, and his answer comes in his own hand — sometimes within the hour, sometimes after days, depending on what else holds his attention. Covenfolk learn quickly: the quill-desk is the Pontifex's door.
