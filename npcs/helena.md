# Helena

![Helena, a portrait](../images/entities/ENT_Helena__portrait__v3.jpg)

*A tall, broad-shouldered, grave young woman, frontal, hands folded, in warm ochre-green scholar's robes with a plain neckline, a lectern behind her; matte Gallen-Kallela portrait.*

### Overt Goals

1. **Write the Founding Text** — a work she hopes can soothe the young-vs-old conflict in the Order.
   → `Lore/LORE_Gilds_and_reform.md`.
2. **Raise Voices** — in spare hours she teaches covenfolk and even neighbours Latin literacy and
   public speaking, believing it lets them take part in covenant affairs.
3. **Reclaim Hermetic Authority** — to prove Bonisagus magi can lead not through control but through
   articulating a shared vision.
