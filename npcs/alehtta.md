# Alehtta

### As the world knows them

A goddess or sorceress of death in the far north — "Pohjola" — of impossible, more-than-mortal beauty and dreadful power. Folktales send heroes north to win a fair maiden from her hall. In learned/Hermetic rumour her name attaches to "Terra Feminarum," a land of men's demise. The stories paint her as evil.
