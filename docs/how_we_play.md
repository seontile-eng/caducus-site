# How We Play — the forum, the storyguide, and you


*New here? This is the one page that explains how the saga actually runs. Read it first; everything else (the Primer, the Charter, your character) makes more sense once you have this.*

## Two places: the forum and this site

There are two homes for the saga, and they do different jobs:

- **The [forum](https://caducus.discourse.group) is where you PLAY.** Every conversation, scene, plan, question, and decision happens there. It's the covenant's voice.
- **This website is where you LOOK THINGS UP.** It's the covenant's ledger — the living state (treasury, vis, offices, library, who's here) and the reference docs, regenerated from the records. Nothing is *done* here; you come here to check a number or re-read a rule.

When in doubt: **do it on the forum, look it up on the site.**

## Talking to the storyguide

The saga is run by an AI storyguide (**ArsBot**) with a human Game Master (**Seon**) behind it. You reach the storyguide on the forum:

- **`@ArsBot`** in a post — mention it with a question or an action, and it answers (usually by the next morning).
- **`!chat`** — opens a **live session** in that thread for a back-and-forth; type **`!done`** (or just go quiet) to end it.
- **In the Character Generation board, just post** — no command needed; saying hello opens a guided character build.
- **PM ArsBot** for anything private. (The GM's own account is *Seon* — a separate inbox the bot can't read, for when you want the human directly.)

You never need a command to be heard in a live scene — just post in the thread.

## How to write a post

A simple convention keeps speech, action, and table-talk apart:

- `"In quotes"` — your **character speaking** aloud.
- `*In italics*` — **narration / action** (what your character does).
- `(In parentheses)` — **out-of-character** (you, the player, talking to the table).

So: *Aulus sets down the cup and studies the redcap.* `"And who sent you?"` `(sorry, slow week — back properly tomorrow)`.

## The golden rule: open play

**Play is open — everyone can read every scene.** What *you* know is not what your *character* knows, and that's fine: the engine tracks who-knows-what, so you never have to hide a thread to protect a secret. Read freely; play your character on what *they'd* know.

Now and then ArsBot will **send you a private note** (a "Quiet Word") — something only your character perceives (an Awareness roll, a Lore insight). You decide, in the open thread, what your character does with it.

Go private (PM **Seon**) only for: admin/personal matters, an agreed character secret, a spoiler-risk question, or consented player-vs-player intrigue.

## Safety

This saga is set on a hard 13th-century frontier and **depicts slavery and other grim realities** as part of its world, never as endorsement. We use **lines and veils** (topics ruled out entirely, and topics that happen "off-screen") and an **X-card** — say the word, in-thread or by PM, and we stop or steer away, no explanation owed. Tell the GM your lines before play if you'd like.

## Your first year (the founding)

Caducus is brand new (the year is 1220), and the founding cohort arrives as **Guests** of the covenant's head, **Aurelius**. Early on, ArsBot will open an **in-character conversation with Aurelius** for your magus: he'll talk over **which office's duties you'd like to take on** and **what project** you'd run for it. **Offices go first-come, first-served** — settle one you want, and it's yours; come late and Aurelius will steer you to a free one. (You hold the duties provisionally as a Guest; who *formally* holds each office is a later story.) This is also how you learn the budget you'll plan against.
