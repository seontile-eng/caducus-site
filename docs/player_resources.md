## Quick reference — the two that trip people up

**The annual cycle & budgeting.** We play one in-game year at a time as a block; all of a year's stories run at once, and you plan *next* year throughout this one. The AI gives you a **projection** of what you and your office will have to work with, so you plan against real numbers. Partway through the year the AI opens a **reconciliation** thread — everyone's planned projects and costs in one place — so the officers can fit their plans to the budget and each other. At the turn of the year the **Spring Council** (played in character) approves the budget. **The budget *proportions* — how much vis/silver/service each office gets — are set a year ahead and locked for the year they govern** (you can't plan a project without knowing its budget), so the Council's re-cutting of the shares takes effect the year *after*. Full version in the Primer; the letter of it is Charter §5–§6.

**Universal Vis (UV).** For *budgeting only*, all vis is treated as one interchangeable currency called Universal Vis, so a budget can be written without pinning exact Arts; it converts to real pawns when spent. Rates (Charter §6.4): in stock **1 UV = 2 Technique = 4 Form** pawns; not in stock **1 UV = 1 Technique = 2 Form**. Stock is assessed the first day of Winter *for budget/salary purposes only*; your plans are valued **at the moment you submit them**, **first-come-first-served**: whoever claims concrete vis first gets it at the cheaper in-stock rate, late posters pay the Mercere replacement rate for the shortfall. (Ursula keeps the books — you just plan in UV.)

**Supply ships.** Two visits a year: **Oculus in early Spring** (through Falke's Baltic-thaw ritual at Arkona) and **Waddenzee in early Autumn** (before the ice closes again). Redcaps ride along. Foot oversees them. The subsidies arrive as **pre-arranged concrete goods** (cloth, iron, salt, grain, tools — requested in advance through Aurelius's twinned quills) plus any £ remainder in **silver coin**. Trade window — Ursula queues purchases against the next visit.

## Also worth knowing (full detail in the Primer)
- **Offices:** the Pontifex (Aurelius) + six body-part officers (Forehead/Heart/Stomach/Groin/Hand/Foot). You'll be offered one early; running its yearly project is part of play.
- **Service:** Members owe 1 season/year, Guests 2. You start as a Guest and become a Member at the Spring 1221 Council.
- **Money:** each office has a yearly budget (no rollover); **no vis tax**; officers earn 1/10 of their project spend — **mundane spend → £ salary, vis spend → vis salary** (dual-currency); 5 pawns of Re/Vi are always reserved for the Aegis.
- **Ownership:** what you make entirely with your own time and resources is yours; what uses covenant vis/resources/service is the covenant's. Discovered vis is the covenant's (you keep a finder's fee).
- **The people:** the covenant includes free covenfolk, hired labourers, the Koroinen clan's labour — and **enslaved people**; the saga treats that as a live moral matter (see Safety).
