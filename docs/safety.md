# Safety Tools — Lines, Veils & the X-Card


Caducus plays a grounded, morally grey saga. The medieval world's brutalities — serfdom and
slavery, crusade and raid violence, religious persecution — and the supernatural's own weight
(the Infernal, the dead, the Fae) are present in the fiction and taken seriously. That's exactly
why these tools exist: naming a limit is never a judgement on anyone, it's ordinary table hygiene,
and it's yours to use whenever you want.

## The tools

Three tiers cover *content*, plus one mechanism that covers *the moment*:

- **Lines** — content that's off the table entirely. A hard stop; the story routes around it, no
  exceptions, no explanation needed, no negotiation. A line is honoured the instant it's named.
- **Veils** — content that may exist in the world but fades to black: referenced, never depicted
  on-screen. The aftermath can be acknowledged; the act itself is not shown.
- **Discuss-before-use** — content that's allowed on-screen, but gets a heads-up and a
  consent-check before a scene actually goes there. Not a hard stop, not an automatic fade — just
  everyone opting in with eyes open.
- **The X-card** *(a different kind of tool — a live pause, not a content category)* — at any
  moment, anyone may pause, skip, or retcon back content that's already happening, no questions
  asked and no reason owed. It covers *anything*, whether or not it was named in advance.

## Using the X-card in play-by-post

Async play needs an async version of the signal. Post a clearly-marked OOC line in the thread —
something like `(X-card: please pull back / skip <X>)` — or PM the GM if you'd rather keep it
private. **Name what to pull back or skip** and that content gets edited or retconned out, the
scene reframes around it, no explanation is asked for, and play continues. **Post a bare card with
nothing named** and the AI stops that thread immediately instead of guessing what to skip, and
hands it to the GM to sort out. Either way, it never argues, negotiates, or narrates through one.

## What's already on the table

A starting draft, confirmed and amended by the table at session zero:

- **Likely veils** (fade to black): sexual content; torture; harm to children; the explicit
  mechanics of slavery and abduction. Present in the world, not depicted on-screen.
- **Likely discuss-before-use**: graphic violence and gore; the Infernal's tempt-and-corrupt beats;
  in-character bigotry of the period. Allowed, but flagged and checked before a scene goes there.
- **Lines**: set by the table — an unstated line is still a line the moment someone names it.

## Setting or changing your own limits

Your personal limits are captured when you join and can be added to or changed at any time — a
limit you name mid-saga takes effect immediately. They're kept private to the GM unless you say
otherwise; a limit that should stop *any* story dead (a hard line) gets folded into the table's
own registered lines so it's enforced automatically, not just remembered.

**Ask the GM (PM Seon) to walk through any of this any time** — to set a limit, to check what's
already registered, or just to talk through how the tools work before you need them.
