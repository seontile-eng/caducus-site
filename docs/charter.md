# CHARTER OF THE COVENANT OF CADUCUS
*Established in the Year of Our Lord, 1220*

> **Player-facing copy.** This is the full Charter your magus is bound by — long and dense; you do
> not need to read it cover to cover. For the plain-language version of the parts that matter day to
> day, read the **Player Primer** ("How Caducus Works"), and you can always ask the AI-GM what the
> Charter says about any particular thing. *(Published mirror; the canonical copy lives in the GM's
> records, and the two are kept identical.)*

## 1. Preamble

We, the undersigned Members of the Order of Hermes, unite under the Covenant of Caducus to advance the Hermetic Arts, defend our Order, and manage our shared resources responsibly.

Essential stipulations (the body text governs in case of conflict):
- The Council of Caducus is composed of all Members. It meets four times each year and decides most matters as Issues of Simple Majority.
- The Pontifex wields a powerful veto, oversees covenant activities, and determines whether Officers receive their annual salaries.
- Six Officers — Forehead, Heart, Stomach, Groin, Hand, and Foot — each control a specific realm of covenant life: accounting, magical resources, internal organization, income, defense, and external relations, respectively.
- At the start of Spring, the Council approves Annual Budgets of mundane, magical, and service-season resources for the Officers based on their proposed Projects.
- Members must provide one season of service per year, Guests two.
- Any discovered vis belongs to the covenant, with the exception of finders' fees.
- Creations remain Private Property if produced solely with personal or privately borrowed resources and time.

## 2. Oath of Covenant
Each prospective Status-Holder swears to serve and protect Caducus, abide by its Charter, uphold obligations to the Order, yield to lawful Council decisions and Officer rulings, attend meetings, and accept discipline for failures.

## 3. Membership and Status
Three categories of Status-Holders: **Members, Guests, Apprentices**. Familiars share their master's Aegis/provisions (not counted separately). **Shield grogs, covenfolk, and companions (consortes) are NOT Status-Holders by default** and fall under the appropriate Officers' mandates.

- **3.1 Admission:** applicants must be Order Members; a current Member sponsors them in the Autumn Council (Simple Majority), effective the following Spring.
- **3.2 Members:** may vote and hold office; **owe one season of service/year (§9.1)**; receive Aegis, shelter, provisions, preferential library access, and a lab (if magi).
- **3.3 Guests:** the Foot may grant/rescind Guest status without a vote before Autumn's end (effective the following Spring, for a year). Guests may attend/speak at Council but have no vote and cannot hold office; **owe two seasons of service/year (§9.1)**; receive an Aegis token, shelter, provisions, library access, lab if magi.
- **3.4 Apprentices:** dwell under their parens' authority; receive Aegis, shelter, provisions, library access. A gauntleted apprentice of Caducus becomes a Guest immediately.
- **3.5 Departure:** Members may resign (forfeiting Covenant Property claims; departing audit by the Forehead). Guests end visits at Winter's close by returning the Aegis token. Death/disappearance/Final Twilight declared by Council (Simple Majority); 2 years generally required for a missing Member.

## 4. Governance

### 4.1 Council
All Members form the Council. Meets four times yearly (first day of each Hermetic season); three Officers (≥3 persons) may call an extraordinary meeting; quorum = half the Members. Rules on all covenant matters, subject to the Code, Peripheral Code, and the Pontifical Veto.

### 4.2 Pontifex
**The Pontifex is NOT an Officer** (held separately, in addition to any Officer posts). Elected Simple Majority, serves indefinitely, not subject to censure; must be a Member.
- **Approval of Self-Allocation:** must approve any season of work or resources an Officer assigns to themselves. **The Pontifex approves their own allocations.**
- **Pontifical Veto:** may nullify any covenant decision/activity; immediate, cannot be overridden.
- **Monitoring:** monthly Officer reports; may audit any non-private activity.
- **Approval of Salaries:** on the last day of Winter decides whether Officers receive full salaries. **The Pontifex's budget (§6.8) may only be used for such salaries.**
- **Immunity:** immune from covenant Discipline. **Rescinding Existential Threat:** may end a Hand-declared Existential Threat (§9.2).

### 4.3 Officers

#### 4.3.1 General Provisions
- **Election/Term:** Officers must be Members, elected Simple Majority, two-year renewable terms; any Member may nominate.
- **Duties/Autonomy:** abide by Council, Charter, Hermetic law; act only for the common benefit of a majority of members; broad discretion within budget and mandate; **pre-emptive Pontifical approval only needed for self-allocations**; must conceive/execute Projects and Ad Hoc Initiatives; subject to retroactive Discipline.
- **Corporeal Precedence:** Forehead > Heart > Stomach > Groin > Hand > Foot (default chain); each Officer outranks others within their mandate; the Stomach mediates unclear cases (the Foot if the Stomach is a party).
- **Delegation/Incapacitation:** an Officer may act personally (Pontifex must approve self-allocations) or delegate; an absent Officer's mandate passes down the Precedence; the Stomach (or Foot) proposes a replacement election.
- **Reporting:** detailed reports ≥10 days before each Council meeting, to the Pontifex, Stomach, and notice board.
- **Multiple Offices:** a Member may hold up to three offices.
- **Officer Salaries (dual-currency):** an Officer earns **one-tenth of their verified expenditure on Projects and Ad Hoc Initiatives completed within the year**, paid on the last day of Winter. Verified by the Stomach (the Foot if the Stomach is verified), subject to Pontifical review. Multi-year Projects count only in the completion year (total expenditure to date). **Mundane spend earns mundane salary: 1/10 of the £ expenditure, paid in pounds (no minimum, not converted to Universal Vis).** **Vis spend earns vis salary: 1/10 calculated in Universal Vis (§6.4), minimum 0.25 Universal Vis (one pawn of Form vis in stock) — lowered from "one pawn" per GM 2026-05-23 — and paid from Concrete Vis in stock, first come first served.** Split per GM 2026-05-28. The Pontifex may withhold any salary; withheld resources revert to the treasury.
- **Awards/Claims:** Officers may grant extra rewards within budget for service beyond obligations (suggested: 1 pawn in-stock Form for short tasks; 4 in-stock Form / 2 in-stock Technique for a dedicated season). Shared exclusive resources: first come, first served (except the Hand's perpetual Aegis priority).
- **Petitions / Overruling:** Members/Guests may petition an Officer; an Officer's in-mandate decision stands unless vetoed by the Pontifex or overruled Simple Majority; disputes mediated by the Stomach (Foot if Stomach involved).

#### 4.3.2 Forehead (Mundane Accounts and Budgets)
Accountant covenfolk; drafts the Preliminary and Finalized **Mundane and Season-of-Service Budgets** before Spring (§6); approves non-standard mundane/service allocations; manages treasury mundane finances (borrowing, lending, acquisitions); maintains the mundane ledger.

#### 4.3.3 Heart (Magical Resources)
Librarian covenfolk; drafts the Preliminary and Finalized **Vis Budgets** before Spring (§6); handles vis harvesting, loans, exchanges, expeditions for new sources; oversees the library; maintains the magical ledger; organizes vis/item storage and security.

#### 4.3.4 Stomach (Internal Relations)
Administers covenfolk not under another Officer (incl. companions/consortes); mediates disputes; arranges non-income, non-military structures (incl. personal labs/sancta); **chairs Council, compiles agendas, records minutes, maintains the Proxy roster**; **verifies Officers' expenditure for salary purposes**.

#### 4.3.5 Groin (Income Sources)
Administers income-source covenfolk; manages income structures/magics; acquires income-generating items/spells.

#### 4.3.6 Hand (Defense and Security)
Military personnel (incl. shield grogs); maintains defenses and protective magics (**incl. the Aegis**), guards property/sancta/personnel/vis sources; expeditions for defense; investigates Charter breaches; maintains internal order.

#### 4.3.7 Foot (External Relations)
Messenger covenfolk; manages external communication; circulates Letters of Invitation to peregrinators and chooses applicants; **grants/removes Guest status**; oversees Redcap services; serves as Tribunal Envoy if needed.

## 5. Projects and Initiatives
- **5.1 Definition:** Projects are pre-planned major Officer endeavours for the common benefit; Ad Hoc Initiatives are spontaneous. Projects are a **budgeting device** (they size each Officer's budget). Activities benefiting only individuals (e.g. Longevity Rituals) cannot be Projects/Initiatives or funded from Officers' Budgets.
- **5.2 Proposals:** announce intended Projects ≥30 days before Spring, with goals, implementation, and estimated mundane/vis/service-season costs.
- **5.3–5.5 Budgeting/Approval:** the Forehead and Heart fold proposals into the Preliminary Annual Budgets; Officers reconcile; Projects in the approved Finalized Draft are carried out; leftover budget must be used as Ad Hoc Initiatives.

## 6. Budgeting
Officers receive resources through the Annual Budget and must not exceed them unless the Council grants Additional Budgets.
- **6.1 Preliminary:** ≥20 days before Spring, the Forehead (mundane + service seasons) and Heart (vis) post Preliminary Annual Budgets; all Officers reconcile the next day.
- **6.2 Council Approval:** 10 days before Spring, the Forehead and Heart co-sponsor a **Finalized Draft Annual Budget** (Simple Majority) containing: a **Season-of-Service Budget**, a **Mundane Budget** (funds, Officer salaries), a **Vis Budget** (incl. salaries), a **Pontifical Salary Budget** (§6.8), and a Plain-Language Summary. At the same Spring Council the Council also **sets the Proportions for the *following* year** (§6.9).
- **6.3 Additional Budgets:** Council may adjust at any meeting (Simple Majority).
- **6.4 Universal Vis:** for budgeting, all vis is interchangeable "Universal Vis," converted to Concrete Vis at: 1 universal = 1 Technique not in stock; 1 = 2 Form not in stock; **1 = 2 Technique in stock; 1 = 4 Form in stock**. Stock assessed on the first day of Winter.
- **6.5 Failure to Budget:** revert to the prior year's allocations, scaled by the Forehead and Heart if total resources differ materially.
- **6.6 Unspent Budget:** **no rollover** — unused budget does not carry forward.
- **6.7 Strategic Vis Reserve:** **five pawns of Rego or Vim must be maintained at all times** for the Aegis; no other claim may reduce the treasury below this.
- **6.8 Pontifical Salary Budget (DUAL-CURRENCY, GM 2026-05-28):** a dedicated allocation set aside each year for Officer salaries (§4.3.1) under the Pontifex's authority (§4.2). **(a) Vis side:** standing UV allocation, Council-set proportion (`office_budget.csv`, Pontifex `vis_budget_UV`). **(b) Mundane side:** auto-sized at year-start = **one-tenth of projected officer mundane project spend** (from the approved Finalized Annual Budget §6.2). If projections are not available, the year's mundane salary budget is sized as **one-tenth of total officer discretionary** (the worst case: every penny of discretionary spent on projects). Sits as a **common pre-allocation** off the allocable pool, like inflation. **May ONLY be used for salary payments**; undisbursed portions on either side revert to the treasury at Winter's end.
- **6.9 Proportions Are Set a Year in Advance and Locked.** The **Proportions** — the shares in which each resource (vis, mundane funds, service seasons) is divided among the Offices — are fixed **one year before the year they govern, and may not be altered for that year.** This is not a formality but a necessity: an Officer cannot conceive a Project *for consumption* without knowing the budget it must fit, and the Council approves those Projects in the Finalized Annual Budget (§6.2); therefore a year's Proportions must already be settled before that year's Projects are drafted. Accordingly, at each Spring Council the Council **(a)** approves the coming year's Budget, whose Proportions were settled the prior year and whose Projects are built upon them, and **(b)** sets the Proportions for the **following** year. Absent a decision, the prior year's Proportions carry forward (§6.5). **Corollary (founding):** the founding Pontifex, in setting the first year's Proportions, thereby also fixes those of the second year (its Projects being drafted during the first against the same shares); the Council assumes this power from the second Spring Council onward.
- **6.10 Running Costs Are Borne by the Offices.** The Annual Budget allocates the covenant's **whole mundane income**, not merely its surplus. Each Officer's mundane allocation must cover the **running costs of the covenfolk and assets under their mandate** (§4.3), the remainder being their **discretionary** budget for Projects and Ad Hoc Initiatives (on which alone salary is reckoned, §4.3.1). Two things sit outside the Offices' allocations, as **common charges borne before allocation**: the upkeep of the **magi themselves** (a right of membership, §3.2), and any cost no Officer commands. The personnel and costs of an established **Income Source are self-contained** — they do not appear among the covenant's running costs; the Source contributes its **net yield** to the common income. **No Officer may commit beyond their allocation**; to exceed it requires an Additional Budget (§6.3). *(Consequence: a cost an Officer chooses to incur — recruiting grogs, hiring a specialist — falls on that Officer's own discretionary, not on the common surplus.)*

## 7. Voting, Agenda, and Meeting Procedures
- **7.1–7.4:** any Member may sponsor a motion (to the Stomach + notice board ≥10 days prior); the Stomach finalizes the agenda, runs debate/amendment, and calls the vote; quorum = half the Members; only Members vote (aye = sigil on palm).
- **7.5 Thresholds:** **Supermajority** = two-thirds of all Members (Charter amendments, Expulsion, Confiscation); **Simple Majority** = majority of Members present with quorum (membership changes, Officer elections, Reprimand/Suspension/Censure/Restitution, Annual/Additional Budgets); the Stomach decides ambiguous categories.
- **7.6 Proxies:** a Member may appoint a standing or single-meeting Proxy (written, to the Stomach); proxies count toward quorum; each attending Member holds at most one Proxy.
- **7.7–7.8:** the Stomach records minutes (stored in the library); passed motions bind unless vetoed or superseded; defeated motions wait until the next meeting.

## 8. Ownership of Resources
- **8.1 Vis:** discovered vis/sources are Covenant Property except finders' fees — **cache:** discoverer keeps half (rounded down) of a non-recurring pocket; **source:** discoverer of a new recurring source receives its **first annual yield** as Private Property. Vis extracted during compulsory service is Covenant Property; in a free season it belongs to the extractor. (Concealing discovered vis → Confiscation/Expulsion.)
- **8.2 Structures:** all land/buildings are Covenant Property; each Member has an inviolate private sanctum; permanent lab upgrades are Covenant Property; the covenant provides each Member-magus a standard lab + sanctum + staff as budget allows; above-standard lab upkeep is paid privately.
- **8.3 Magical Items:** anything produced with covenant vis/resources/compulsory service is Covenant Property unless granted to an individual (Simple Majority).
- **8.4 Private Property:** items made entirely with personal/privately-borrowed resources remain private; using core infrastructure (Aegis, shelter, provisions, library, lab space) does not convert products; register private property with the Forehead within 30 days (unregistered → presumed Covenant Property in audits/Confiscation).

## 9. Obligations
- **9.1 Compulsory Service:** **Members owe one season of service per year; Guests owe two.** Officers assign these seasons (assignee may request scheduling; first come, first served among Officers; no Private Property demanded). The assigning Officer judges completion. If an Officer fails to assign a season, the obligation lapses; if the Status-Holder obstructs it, they fail their obligation. **Minor Requests:** up to two per Officer per season (≤1 day, no personal vis/peril), max four across all Officers per season.
- **9.2 Emergency Assistance:** any three Officers (≥3 persons) may declare an **Existential Threat**, obligating all to defend; chain of command Hand > Forehead > Heart > Stomach > Groin > Foot; the Pontifex may end it.
- **9.3 Conduct:** must not endanger Caducus through reckless dealings.
- **9.4 Reporting Violations:** report breaches/threats to the Hand immediately (else the Stomach or Pontifex).
- **9.5 Confidentiality:** must not disclose the Charter, Council deliberations, or covenant defenses outside the covenant without the Foot's approval.

## 10. Discipline
- **10.1–10.2:** the Council may impose Discipline for harmful conduct: **Reprimand** / **Suspension** (Simple Majority); **Confiscation** of Private Property by the Hand / **Expulsion** (Supermajority).
- **10.3 Officers:** **Censure** (Simple Majority — loses office immediately); **Restitution** from the Officer's Private Property; resignation does not absolve prior acts.
- **10.4 Due Process:** the accused may speak in their defense before any penalty.

## 11. Amendment and Dissolution
- **11.1 Amendment:** proposed ≥40 days before a meeting; **two-thirds of all Members** to amend (Supermajority); limited-term deviations are handled as Amendments.
- **11.2 Dissolution:** two-thirds of all Members; after one year, remaining assets allocated by the final Council or passed to Tribunal care.

## 12. Final Ratification
Ratified by the Council of Caducus in Spring of 1220.
