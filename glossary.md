# Glossary

*The Year 1220 — the founding year*

**Art** — one of the fifteen kinds of Hermetic magic (five Techniques like *Creo*, ten Forms like *Ignem*). Scores grow with study.

**Aegis of the Hearth** — the great yearly warding ritual over the whole covenant. Until it is cast, the covenant is magically unguarded.

**Aura** — the ambient magical power of a place; it strengthens magic worked inside it.

**Covenant** — the magi's settlement and institution -- our home, Caducus.

**Covenfolk** — everyone who lives and works at the covenant besides the magi.

**Gauntlet** — the test that ends an apprenticeship; a magus's 'graduation'.

**Pawn** — the unit of raw magical power (vis). Spent on rituals, enchantments, and study.

**Sanctum** — a magus's private laboratory and quarters; entering uninvited is a deadly insult.

**Season** — the unit of a magus's time. Each year holds four; each is spent on ONE thing -- study, lab work, adventure, or service.

**Summa / Tractatus** — the two kinds of study book: a summa teaches up to its Level; a tractatus gives one burst of insight (its Quality).

**Strategic Reserve** — the Rego and Vim vis the Charter requires us to hold back for the Aegis and emergencies.

**Tribunal** — the regional assembly of covenants (ours: Novgorod) -- and the once-in-seven-years meeting where its law is made.

**Universal Vis (UV)** — the covenant's internal accounting unit for vis of any Art.

**Vis** — raw magical power, condensed in physical things -- the saga's most precious resource.

**The Order of Hermes** — the society of magi to which we all belong; its law is the Code.
