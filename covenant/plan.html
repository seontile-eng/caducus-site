Plan Your Season

The recurring task — spend your four season-slots against the covenant’s purse and the year’s calendar.

Your four seasons

Each magus gets four season-slots a year to spend — and the whole year is played at once, so you plan all four together. This page is what you plan against.

The one rule that trips everyone: a distraction doesn’t cost a slot

  • A quiet season — lab work, study, writing, extracting vis — is one slot, resolved by the engine.
  • A short adventure (up to a month in the fiction) is a distraction: it costs no slot. You keep the full result of your planned lab/study work and may instead take Adventure XP — your choice.
  • Only a long story (more than a month) eats the season (−1/3 of the season’s advancement per full month).

So short stories layer on for free; only a long expedition spends a slot. Classify each intended story as short or long when you plan — only the long ones reduce your four slots.

The covenant’s purse, 1221

What your projects draw on.
  • Mundane surplus to split across offices: £54.53 (income £120.0 − upkeep £65.47).
  • Vis: 20 pawns/year incoming + 30 carried, less the 5-pawn Strategic Reserve held back.
  • Each office’s free budget is on Offices & Service; officer salary = 1/10 of project spend; no vis tax, no rollover.

When to plan — the year’s rhythm

A whole in-game year is played as one block of real time (its four seasons run at once). Within that block:

  1. Early: planning threads open on Covenant Planning — start shaping next year’s seasons + any office project.
  2. Weeks 3–5: the budget reconciliation thread — fit every office’s projects to its share.
  3. Week 6: the storyguide starts steering open stories toward a close.
  4. Week 8: wrap-up — last year is closed, the Spring Council approves next year’s budget, and the new year launches.

Don’t wait for the deadline: the earlier your four seasons are settled, the more they shape the budget. Plan on the Covenant Planning board; look the numbers up here.
