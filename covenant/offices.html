The Covenant of Caducus  ·  Anno 1220

Offices & Service

The six offices — their mandates and holders, and each one’s discretionary budget and current project.

Six offices

The covenant runs on six offices named for parts of a body (precedence Forehead › Heart › Stomach › Groin › Hand › Foot). Above them sits the Pontifex — not an Officer himself (Charter §4.2), but the holder of a veto, the approver of officers’ self-allocations, and the one who decides whether officers are paid. Each office bears the running costs of the covenfolk it commands first; what’s left is its discretionary budget for projects. As a founding Guest you request an office’s duties at character creation; an uncontested request is granted outright, and where more than one founder wants the same office Aurelius decides by their first-year proposals (see How We Play) — who formally holds each is a later story.
