The Covenant of Caducus  ·  Anno 1220

Offices & Service

The seven offices — their mandates and holders, and each one’s discretionary budget and current project.

Seven offices

The covenant runs on seven offices — the Pontifex above six body-part Officers (precedence Forehead › Heart › Stomach › Groin › Hand › Foot). Each office bears the running costs of the covenfolk it commands first; what’s left is its discretionary budget for projects. As a founding Guest you’re offered an office’s duties early (first come, first served — see How We Play); who formally holds each is a later story.

Pontifex

Sits above the six — wields the veto, approves salaries and self-allocations (not an Officer).

  • Budget (1221): £0.0 free for projects · 1.75 UV free · 1 service season(s)
