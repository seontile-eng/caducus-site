# Offices & Service

*The Year 1220 — the founding year*

The covenant runs on **seven offices** — the Pontifex above six body-part Officers (precedence Forehead › Heart › Stomach › Groin › Hand › Foot). Each office bears the running costs of the covenfolk it commands first; what's left is its **discretionary** budget for projects. As a founding Guest you're offered an office's *duties* early (first come, first served — see [How We Play](../start-here.html)); who *formally* holds each is a later story.

Sits above the six — wields the veto, approves salaries and self-allocations (not an Officer).
