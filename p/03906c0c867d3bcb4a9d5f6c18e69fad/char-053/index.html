Background

Concept

Intellego/Mentem mind-mage; porpoise-shapeshifter (Ex Miscellanea)

Story

Adolfus was a foundling raised among the covenfolk of a lower-Rhine covenant, which is why the mundane world still feels foreign to him. His parens belonged to an Ex Miscellanea line of coast-witches who could wear the shape of a porpoise; under their teaching he learned to read the moods of the sea as readily as the thoughts behind a man's smile. The gift came doubled with a curse -- at the turning of the tide, or in the grip of strong feeling, he slips into the porpoise against his will. Gentle, slight, and unable to bear another's pain, he is forever drawn to ease it. The sea is his true home; on dry land, and worst of all inland, he is uneasy and diminished. Since his apprenticeship a creature of the deep -- a grey seal that is more than a seal -- has followed wherever he swims. Newly Gauntleted, he has come to Oculus Septentrionalis to join the Caducus peregrination, and the cogs sail in the deep of winter.
